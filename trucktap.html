What it does

Restock the van in taps, not texts.

A two-sided workflow: simple for the tech in the field, complete for the shop behind them.

01

Tap what you used

A visual parts grid built for gloves and a phone mount. The tech taps used items instead of typing or calling.

02

Send one restock list

The tech submits once. No separate texts, voicemails, or photos of a handwritten list.

03

Live shop pick-list

The shop sees a morning restock board grouped by van, with progress as lines get picked.

04

Consolidated supplier order

Combine demand across vans into one supplier order instead of scrambling across three purchase lists.

05

Works with no signal

Local-first data means taps are saved on the device. The queue syncs when the connection returns.

06

Team roles & sync

Accounts, workspaces, and roles for techs and shop staff, with real multi-device sync behind them.

Built for the field

The job doesn't wait for signal.

A basement, a parking garage, a rural service call — the van still needs restocking. TruckTap was designed offline-first from the start, not patched to tolerate it.

  • Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, and general service fleets
  • Shop and warehouse runners building daily pick-lists
  • Parts managers consolidating supplier orders
  • Teams that need a Windows app and a browser app from one system
