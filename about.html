The studio

We build for the people
using the software.

Vybelive LLC is an independent software studio. We started by building our own products for dealerships, field service teams, and secure facilities — and learned what separates software that gets used from software that gets abandoned.

Why Vybelive

Real constraints beat polished demos.

Our products live in places with bad Wi-Fi, tired users, compliance questions, and a key that absolutely cannot go missing. That shaped how we build everything: local-first where it matters, clear interfaces, and data that doesn't disappear.

It's the same approach we bring to contract work. We care about installers that don't fail, records that hold up, and a workflow a person can use at 6 p.m. without training.

You work directly with the people designing and writing the software. No agency layer, no hand-off to a junior team.

What we believe

Three rules we don't bend.

01 / PRACTICAL

Useful over impressive

The best feature is the one that removes a step from someone's day. We optimize for the person doing the work, not the demo.

02 / OWNED

Ship it and stand behind it

Launch isn't the finish line. We support what we build, fix what breaks, and keep the installers and updates working.

03 / HONEST

No black boxes

Clear scope, visible progress, direct answers. If something is a bad idea or will take longer, you hear it early.
