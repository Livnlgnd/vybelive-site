What it does

Verified access, without the round trip.

Recognition, liveness, matching, and audit all happen locally — in milliseconds, not uploads.

01

On-device recognition

Face detection and identity matching run in the app. The kiosk keeps working with no internet connection.

02

Liveness checks

A photo held up to the camera shouldn't open a door. Liveness and quality checks gate every match.

03

Encrypted templates

Identities are stored as encrypted templates. Raw camera frames are never uploaded or retained as images.

04

Fully standalone

The kiosk is complete on its own. A site does not need a server, an account, or a live connection to operate.

05

Optional central sync

For multi-site operators, an optional control plane can distribute encrypted identities and collect audit events.

06

iOS companion

Sentry Companion pairs over local Wi-Fi to show the site audit stream and kiosk inventory from an iPhone.

Privacy by architecture

No images. No cloud dependency.

Most biometric systems ask you to trust a vendor's cloud. Sentry removes that conversation: the camera, the model, the template, and the audit trail stay on hardware you control.

  • Camera frames are processed locally and never uploaded
  • Identity templates are encrypted at rest
  • Access decisions continue through network outages
  • Audit events live on the kiosk and sync only if configured
  • Built for security integrators deploying on customer sites

How it works

Enroll once. Verify locally. Keep the record.

  1. 01

    Enroll

    Capture a consented identity sample and store only the encrypted template.

  2. 02

    Verify

    The kiosk checks liveness, image quality, and a local match in milliseconds.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Grant or deny on-device. The door does not wait on a server response.

  4. 04

    Audit

    Events land in the local audit stream; central sync is optional, never required.
