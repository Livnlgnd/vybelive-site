KeyAudit Pro

Physical key tracking & custody

Keys walk out of dealerships and property offices every day. KeyAudit Pro makes every handoff accountable — QR-labeled fobs, one-tap checkout, overdue alerts, and a tamper-evident chain of custody that holds up to an audit.

  • QR label printing and scan-to-checkout
  • Overdue and missing-key alerts
  • Full custody history by key, person, and site
  • Role-based access for teams and managers

TruckTap

Van & truck restock, in one tap

Field techs shouldn't dictate parts over the phone. TruckTap lets them tap what they used on the van, send one restock list, and get back to the job. The shop sees a live pick-list and a consolidated supplier order — online or completely offline.

  • Tap-to-restock for technicians in the field
  • Live shop pick-list and supplier order builder
  • Local-first data with real multi-device sync
  • Windows desktop app plus installable PWA

Sentry Access Control

Offline-first biometric access

Access control that keeps working when the network doesn't. Sentry runs facial recognition, liveness checks, and encrypted identity matching entirely on-device — no camera images ever leave the kiosk. Built for security integrators and sites that can't afford a cloud dependency.

  • On-device recognition with liveness detection
  • Encrypted templates — no images uploaded
  • Works fully standalone; optional central sync
  • Windows kiosk app plus iOS companion

Process

No mystery.
No black box.

  1. 01

    Scope

    A short call and a written plan. We define the smallest version worth shipping, the risks, and a real timeline.

  2. 02

    Design

    Interface direction and key flows before heavy code. You see and react to the product early, while changes are cheap.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly builds you can actually use. Progress is demoed, not described. Feedback goes straight into the next increment.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    Deployment, stores, installers, docs, and handover. Stay on for maintenance, or take the keys and run.
